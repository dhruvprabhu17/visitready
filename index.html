The problem

A follow-up date, but no word on what to do before it

A patient is told “come back after 2 weeks”, but not whether a new test or an old report is needed at that visit. Many only find out at the clinic, after travelling, waiting and paying.

27%
of outpatient out-of-pocket spend in India goes on consultation fees and on travel, food and lodging.

12.4% consultation fees plus 14.6% travel, food and lodging (NSS 2017-18; Ambade et al., JAMA Network Open 2022). A repeat trip pays these twice. This is the share of spend a repeat trip doubles, not a measure of how many visits are wasted.

Who is affected

Patients with diabetes, thyroid, blood pressure or kidney conditions; people travelling in from other towns or suburbs; older and first-time patients holding handwritten slips. Mumbai first, then other cities.

What is missing today

Slips rarely say what to bring or do beforehand. Booking and records apps fix the slot and store reports but do not say if a test is due. Calling reception means hold times.

Why it matters

A wasted visit costs a fare, a day's wages and a second fee. The clinic spends a consultation without the data it needed. For long-term conditions, it also delays treatment decisions.

Forgetfulness and no reminders topped the reasons in a 2026 study of 100 patients who missed outpatient visits (Int J Community Med Public Health).

How it works

From the paper you already have to one clear answer

Input

A photo of the note or discharge summary, plus the visit date.

Process

OCR and AI read the tests and dates. The patient confirms what was read. Doctor-reviewed rules check whether a test is due.

Output

A plain answer: test needed, not needed, or ask the clinic. With it: which test, by when, fasting, and which old reports to carry.

What makes it different. It works on the paper patients already have, and it says “ask the clinic” instead of guessing.

Social impact

Families stop losing a day's wages and a fee to a trip that could not help. Clinics spend consultations on decisions, not rebooking. Supports SDG 3, Good Health and Well-being.

Sustainability

Free for patients, always. Revenue from a small clinic subscription and optional lab-partner bookings. We never sell patient data.

Scale-up

One condition group at a few Mumbai clinics, then one condition at a time as doctors approve the rules, then other cities. Later, link to ABHA records.
